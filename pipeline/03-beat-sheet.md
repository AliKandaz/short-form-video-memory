## Set
A quiet suburban street corner in early winter: cream houses with terracotta roofs and blue shutters, lawns dusted with snow, bare
trees, a curving asphalt road and a concrete sidewalk. In the foreground the ground at the sidewalk is sliced open like a cake toward
the camera, showing clean layers: snow and dark topsoil, a pale frozen band glittering with ice crystals (the frost layer), brown clay,
grey gravel, and at the bottom a thick blue-grey water main running left to right.
Hero camera: elevated three-quarter view facing the cut, the pipe or hydrant in the centre third, houses behind.

Hydrant (lock this description): a classic American fire hydrant, squat cast-iron body painted red, a domed top with a five-sided
operating nut, one large outlet facing the street and two smaller side outlets with chained caps. The top is red until B8, then light blue.
