## World (Hidden By Design)
A small sunny coastal town: pale sandy beach and turquoise sea in front, a green hill behind with cream houses, terracotta roofs and blue
shutters. The ground and the sea are sliced open toward the camera like a cake: translucent cyan water above a sandy seabed with rocks and
seagrass, and the pipes running under the seabed and the beach.
Hero camera (S0, END): elevated three-quarter view of the sliced coast; the seabed cutaway and the pipe mouth in the lower third, the beach
and the plant site in the centre third, the town on the hill in the upper third.
Hero prop (lock): one small bright yellow fish with a single blue stripe, the recurring character (S0, INTAKE, CAP, DIFFUSER, END).

translucent cyan water, tidy and uncluttered, vertical 9:16 composition with the subject in the centre third, no text, no letters, no
numbers, no captions, no logos, no watermark.
