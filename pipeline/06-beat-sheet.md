## World (Classic look)
A sunny desert canyon diorama: a block of rose-red sandstone mountain cut open toward the camera like a cake, its cut faces showing
soft horizontal bands of red, pink and cream rock. A narrow winding slot canyon (the Siq) runs in from the lower left and opens onto
a small flat plaza of pale golden sand in the middle of the frame; behind it rises a tall, flat, sheer cliff face that faces the
camera (where the facade is carved). Bright blue sky across the top.
Hero camera: slightly elevated three-quarter view looking at the cliff face; the cliff fills the upper-middle of the frame, the plaza
the lower-middle, the canyon enters from the lower left, and the cut front face of the block runs along the bottom (where the
cistern is revealed).
