## World (Classic look)
A sunny crash-test pad diorama: a square slab of smooth light-grey concrete with white lane lines and yellow-and-black hazard stripes
along its edges, short bright green grass around it, two tall camera poles with floodlights at the far corners.
- OLD: a 1959 American full-size four-door sedan, long and low, with huge horizontal tail fins, a wide chrome grille and chrome bumpers,
  pastel turquoise with a cream upper body. No badges. A plain light-grey crash-test dummy with black-and-yellow round target marks drives
  it, with no seat belt.
- NEW: a 2009 mid-size four-door sedan with smooth rounded lines and slim headlights, glossy cherry red. No badges. The locked mascot
  (3.4) drives it, belted, in his yellow hard hat.
- Both cars are cutaways: roof panels and side windows removed like convertibles, windshields kept, so we look down into both cabins.
- Hero camera (to test on S0): high above the pad at a steep angle; the old car in the bottom half pointing up (tail fins nearest the
  camera), the new car in the top half pointing down at it (the mascot faces the camera), slightly offset sideways so the driver's sides
  overlap, front bumpers meeting in the middle. Side by side, two cars come out tiny in a 9:16 frame; stacked, they fill it. If S0 does
  not read, fall back to the usual raised three-quarter view with the cars on a diagonal.
- Labels go in the left margin (the right side has TikTok's buttons). Captions at ~52% would sit on the impact point; set their height
  after Gate C.
