# 09 Lightning: beat sheet (Middle tier, Cutaway look, the first video with the travelling camera)

Idea C3 (section 2), "What Lightning Does Inside Your House". Script approved at Gate A on 15 Nov 2026 with opening B (the cold
claim): 270 words, est. ~77 s at 3.5 words/s, loop ending. Titles: YouTube "What Lightning Does Inside Your House" (the user took
the recommendation); TikTok "What does lightning actually do inside your house? ⚡". Comment question: "Do you shower during a
storm? Yes or no."

Est. seconds = words / 3.5. Real timings come from the narration (Step 6) and replace these; clip lengths are set from them.
Look (3.4): one sliced-open set seen by a travelling camera (Rules 2, 3 and 7); every line is a clip, no planned holds; every
still is an edit of the wide still or a neighbouring position; the storm is a re-grade of the daylight wide; captions ALL CAPS
~57 px at ~75% down; solid white arrows and one thick ring; no labels.

## Set
- **Place:** a two-storey suburban house sliced open like a dollhouse (its whole front wall removed), on a square block of ground
  cut straight down like a cake. Cream walls, a terracotta tile roof, white tiles, pale wood floors. A strip of lawn and a strip of
  grey street along the front edge.
- **Attic:** wooden rafters under the roof, a white electrical cable running along them and down into the walls.
- **Upstairs right:** a bathroom: a white bathtub with a chrome shower head over it, and a copper water pipe running up inside the
  cut right-hand wall to the shower. A small yellow rubber duck on the tub's rim (in the water from B17 on).
- **Upstairs left:** a small bedroom (bed and window): filler, nothing happens there.
- **Downstairs:** a living room with a sofa, a floor lamp and wall outlets; on its left wall a grey electrical panel with its door
  open.
- **Outside:** a wooden power pole at the left front corner of the block, with a crossarm and a grey can transformer, and one black
  service cable from its top to the upper left corner of the house.
- **Below ground:** clean soil layers; the copper water pipe comes in under the lawn to the right foundation corner and rises into
  the right-hand wall.
- **Added by the fixes:** a short metal rod on the right end of the ridge; a thick braided copper cable down the right outside
  corner into the soil; a copper ground rod in the soil at the right corner (fix 1). Copper straps clamping the water pipe, a green
  ground wire from the house and the ground rod together at the right foundation (fix 2). A small grey box beside the panel with a
  green wire down into the floor (fix 3).
- **Grade:** the whole video is the storm: blue-grey storm light, dark clouds, rain, warm lights on inside. It is a re-grade of the
  daylight wide (Rule 4); the daylight wide never appears on screen.
- **What glows:** only the bolts and the current, a white-blue streak and glow along cables and pipes. Nothing else glows.
- **Nobody in the house.** No hands are needed. The duck is the joke's prop.

- **C15, the climax:** Bo → W1B (middle) → Gg, 6 s: it flies between three positions and carries the bolt down the cable, which
  section 2 names as the thing to prove first (electricity is harder to animate than water).
- Both measured with `camera_path.py` and `check_video.py` next to the power-lines video's numbers (66% camera moving, 0.46 px
  parallax, a big move every 4 s; 70% of the seconds moving inside the picture).
