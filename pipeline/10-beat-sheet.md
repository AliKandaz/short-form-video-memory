## Set
- **Place:** a small, modern, two-level research building shown as a cutaway model: its whole front wall is removed like a
  dollhouse. It stands on a square block of ground cut straight down like a cake, with plain soil layers below (the lower ~40%
  of the wide frame). White walls, pale grey floors, brushed stainless steel, soft daylight plus cool white ceiling lights.
- **Left, ground floor: the cooling room.** Double doors in the left wall; a heart monitor on a wheeled stand beside them; a
  steel operating table in the middle (empty at first); an IV pole with two bags of pale-blue liquid; a small pump cart with
  a round rotor; a white counter with a lab tray: a clear vial of water in a rack, a frosty red strawberry on a small white
  dish, a vial of pale-blue antifreeze, and a small open steel flask.
- **Left, upper floor:** a small plain monitoring office (filler; nothing happens there).
- **Right, double height: the storage hall.** Tall stainless-steel cylinder tanks about twice a person's height, in rows going
  back. The front-left tank is sliced open from top to bottom, its front half removed: a thick double steel wall with a gap
  between the two skins (the vacuum), clear liquid nitrogen almost to the top under rolling white fog, and inside it four long
  white sealed pods standing upside down (rounded head end at the bottom) around a middle column of small white canisters
  stacked one above the other. Beside it, tank 2 with a round lid. A steel catwalk with a railing runs along the tank tops at
  the upper-floor level, with a small overhead hoist above tank 2. An insulated silver hose runs from a tall white nitrogen
  tank outside the right wall up to the catwalk. At the back of the row, the oldest tank, duller and crusted with frost.
- **"You":** one long white sealed pod (no face, no skin, nothing written on it) that travels the whole story: on the gurney in
  a tub of ice, on the table with tubes, on the hoist, into tank 2.
- **Added by the story:** the gurney with a white tub (B1), ice and the pump tubes (B3), the antifreeze in the tubes (B7), the
  vial in the steaming flask (B8), the pod on the hoist (B11), a small worker in a blue coverall, gloves and a face shield with
  the hose (B15), a steel coffee thermos and a steaming cup on the catwalk rail (B15).
- **Grade:** bright soft daylight all the way through, except B13: the hall lights go out and come back (a re-grade of the same
  picture, Rule 4). Nothing glows except the lights.
- **Nobody hurt, no faces, no names, no logos, no text** (section 2 watch-outs). The first man is not named; Alcor is not named
  or shown.
