Replies ready:
- "Has anyone been brought back?" No. Not one person has been revived; the whole idea is a bet that future medicine can.
- "Why not just freeze them?" Ice crystals tear the cells. They swap the blood for antifreeze so the body sets like glass
  (vitrification) instead of turning to ice.
- "What if the power goes out?" Nothing. The tanks are vacuum-insulated and don't plug in; a crew tops up the nitrogen as it
  boils away.
