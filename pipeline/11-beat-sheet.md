## Set
- **Place:** a Japanese castle on a steep hill, shown as a cutaway model. The hill rises from a flat plain; a moat of translucent
  cyan water runs round its foot; a small town of grey-tiled wooden houses fills the plain round the moat. Everything stands on a
  square block of ground cut straight down like a cake: its front face shows clean, even layers of brown soil and grey rock (the
  lower ~40% of the wide frame stays plain for the captions). The right-hand face of the block is cut too, through the hill.
- **On the hilltop:** the main tower: a tall wooden castle tower with five tiers of curved grey-tiled roofs and pointed gables, on
  a high base of fitted grey stone, with two smaller towers beside it and a small walled courtyard at its foot. The back-right
  quarter of the main tower is cut away like a dollhouse, so its floors show from the right (positions TOWER and TOP).
- **Walls:** high stone walls round the hilltop and partway down the slope, each curving up like an open fan (gentle at the
  bottom, almost vertical at the top), with earthen walls capped with grey tiles along their tops; a long wooden gallery runs
  behind one wall-top wall (position INSIDE).
- **Stage ladder:**
  - S0: the tower and wall-top walls are bare dark-brown timber and brown earth; a plain gate at the foot of the hill; a straight
    stone path from it straight up the slope to the tower; plain wall-top walls with no openings; an empty storehouse by the
    courtyard; no well. (The fan-curved stone walls are there from the start.)
  - S1: the path winds back and forth up the slope in switchbacks between low walls (B3–B4).
  - S2: a wall stands in front of the gate partway up, so the path turns sharply; gun barrels in slots above it (B5–B6).
  - S3: a chute at the top of the curved wall opens and drops stones (B9; a passing state, the chute closes again).
  - S4: holes in the wall-top walls: circles, triangles and squares (B10–B11).
  - S5: the tower and the wall-top walls coated in smooth white plaster (B12–B14).
  - S6: a round stone-lined well shaft from the courtyard down through the cut hill to cyan water; the storehouse stacked with
    straw rice bales and sacks of salt (B15–B16). **The finished castle; the last frame.**
- **Grades:** bright soft daylight, except B21–B23 (1945): night, re-graded from the day stills, the town on the plain burning
  orange with smoke, the white tower draped in black nets; day again for B24–B25 (Rule 4).
- **People:** small anonymous figures only: a column of foot soldiers in dark armour with spears (no faces, no banners, no flags,
  no crests); in B20 a small man in a dark kimono holding a paper and three workers with long ladders. Nobody is hurt: stones
  bounce down the wall while the climbers slide back; arrows hit wood; the bombers are distant dark shapes high in the sky.
- **No text, names, flags or insignia anywhere** (no Japanese or American flags, no markings on the planes).
