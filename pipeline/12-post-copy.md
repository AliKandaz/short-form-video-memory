Replies ready:
- "It's sewer gas" → Not the ones that blow in big cities: those are power vaults. Salt water eats the cable insulation, an arc
  cooks it into smoke and gas, and one more spark lights it.
- "How high can they go?" → In Boston in 2017 one went at least 50 feet up. Nobody was hurt.
- "Why not bolt them down?" → A sealed lid just lets more pressure build. Holes let the gas out early, and latches let a lid lift
  a little and settle back.
- "Why are they round?" → A round lid can't fall through its own hole.
- "How often does this happen?" → New York averages about two thousand smoking manholes a year, and a few dozen explosions.
