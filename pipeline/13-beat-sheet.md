## Set
- **Place:** a suburban back yard on a cool late-November afternoon, shown as a cutaway model, seen in depth: the cut face of the
  ground at the front, then the lawn (green-brown grass, a few fallen orange leaves), then a wooden deck with three steps down to
  the lawn, then the back of a one-storey house with pale grey siding; a bare maple and a plain wooden fence at the sides.
  Everything stands on a block of ground cut straight down like a cake toward the camera: its front face shows dark topsoil over
  brown earth, with nothing buried in it. The lower ~40% of the wide frame is the plain cut face, for the captions.
- **The house:** its back wall is cut away like a dollhouse, so two rooms show behind the deck: the kitchen (a white fridge, a
  stove with pots on it, an oven, a counter) and the living room beside it (a sofa, a flat TV on the wall). An empty folding lawn
  chair stands on the deck (the uncle's).
- **The fryer**, out on the lawn halfway between the deck and the cut face, far from the house: a tall stainless-steel pot on a
  three-legged steel stand over a round cast-iron ring burner; the pot's front half is cut away like a slice of cake, so the oil
  inside shows as if through glass; a white propane tank beside it on the grass with a black hose to the burner; a steel lifting
  hook holding the turkey on a vertical rack. No brand names anywhere.
- **Stage ladder:**
  - S0: a frozen turkey (frosted white skin, chunks of clear ice in its cavity) hangs on the hook an inch above calm, shimmering
    golden oil; a ring of blue flames under the pot. **First frame (ST0).**
  - S1 (passing; B1-B6): the boil-over. Outside: the bird drops in at ~0.5 s, oil foams up over the rim and runs down onto the
    flames, fire flares up the pot and rolls up into a fireball (ST1, H1, BU0). Inside the pot: the frozen bird under the oil,
    ice in its cavity, clear drops falling from it (IN0); at the bottom, big steam bubbles swelling and bursting (IN1).
  - S2 (B7): in the kitchen, the bird on a tray in the open fridge: frosted (K0), then thawed and patted dry, crumpled paper
    towels beside it (K1).
  - S3 (B8-B9): the burner unlit; the pot full of clear water with the thawed bird on its rack under it (R0); the bird lifted out
    above the pot, dripping, the water a few inches lower, a strip of blue tape on the pot's inside wall at the waterline (R1).
  - S4 (B10-B11): the pot holds golden oil up to the tape; the burner lit; a few drops of oil running down the outside of the pot
    toward the flames (BU1); then the flames out and the thawed bird half lowered into calm oil, which stays well below the rim
    (PT2).
  - S5 (B12-B14): the deck empty but for the lawn chair (D0); the fryer far out on the lawn, the bird down in the pot, a wisp of
    steam rising (H0, W0); pots steaming on the kitchen stove (W0).
  - S6 (B15): the living-room TV shows a fryer erupting in a fireball in an empty parking lot, a red fire engine with no
    lettering parked behind it and two tiny firefighters in tan gear standing well back; no text and no ticker on the screen.
    **Last frame: S0 again (ST0, reused)**: the uncle's frosted bird on the hook above the oil, the burner lit.
- **Grade:** soft, warm late-autumn afternoon light; the oil golden; the burner's flames blue; the fire orange; the kitchen and
  living room warmly lit.
- **Water:** clear water only in the pot during the water test (R0, R1); everything else is dry (the STYLE line without its water
  clause, as for video 12).
- **No people, no text, no logos:** nobody in the yard or the house; the only figures are the two tiny firefighters inside the TV
  picture; no lettering on the propane tank, the fryer, the fire engine or anywhere else.
