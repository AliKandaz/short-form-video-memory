Replies ready:
- "Why not just fry it longer?" → The ice melts, the water sinks to the hot bottom and flashes to steam about 1,700 times its size.
  That's what throws the oil over the rim. Thaw it in the fridge first: about a day for every 4–5 pounds.
- "How much oil do I need?" → Put the bird in the empty pot, cover it with water, lift it out and mark the line. That's your oil.
  Leave a few inches of space at the top.
- "What do you do if it catches?" → Never water. If you can do it safely, shut off the gas; use a fire extinguisher, and call 911.
- "Can I do it in the garage when it's cold?" → No: outside, on flat ground, away from the house, the deck and the garage.
- "Is Thanksgiving really the worst day?" → Yes, the No. 1 day of the year for home cooking fires in the US: about 1,446 of them on
  Thanksgiving 2023 (NFPA).
