## Set
- **Place:** a nuclear power plant's natural-draft cooling tower on a bright, cool, clear autumn day, shown as a cutaway model.
  A tall hyperbolic tower of pale grey concrete with faint horizontal pour lines, narrowing to a waist about two-thirds of the
  way up and flaring out again to its round mouth, stands on a ring of slanted concrete legs with daylight between them; under
  it a round concrete pond of translucent cyan water. Beside it a calm river with grassy banks; at one side a plain, boxy grey
  plant building with no markings, no dome and no chimney (the turbine hall, never the reactor). Behind the tower, in a row
  along the river, three more cooling towers, whole, each with a white cloud rising from its mouth. Everything stands on a deep
  block of ground cut straight down like a cake toward the camera: its cut face shows even layers of soil and, buried in them,
  the big pipe from the plant building, cut open lengthwise so its rushing cyan water shows, running under the tower.
- **The cut (from ~0.5 s on):** the near half of the tower is cut away from its mouth down to the ground, like a slice of cake,
  so its inside shows. From the bottom up: the pond, with rain pouring into it all over; the fill, a wide layer of stacked
  thin white plastic sheets spanning the tower a little above the legs, water running down them in thin films and raining off
  their undersides; just above the fill, a grid of pipes with nozzles spraying water down onto it; just above the sprays, a
  layer of grey chevron baffles (the drift eliminators); in the middle, the big riser pipe coming up out of the ground from the
  plant's pipe and feeding the spray grid, cut open lengthwise so the water surging up it shows. Above the baffles: nothing,
  just the curved inner face of the concrete shell rising to the bright mouth, with faint mist drifting up it. The cut edge of
  the shell shows a thin wall of concrete with steel bars in it.
- **The duck:** one mallard paddling in the pond near the cut edge, under the rain (small in BA0; the joke's close-up is DU0).
- **Stage ladder:**
  - S0: the tower whole and closed, its white cloud rolling out of the top. **First frame (ST0) and last frame.**
  - S1 (~0.5 s to the end): the near half cut away; the rain, the sprays, the riser surging, mist rising, the cloud at the mouth.
    Passing states shown in the clips only: the mist thickening and hanging round the sprays (B7); drops carried up and caught
    by the baffles (B14).
  - S-storm (B12, RO1): the row of three whole towers under a dark sky in a gale, heavy rain driving sideways (the 1965
    re-grade); in the clip they buckle and fall.
  - Back to S1 for B13-B17, and to S0 for the last frame: the near half closes up again as the camera settles on ST0's framing.
- **Grade:** bright, soft, cool daylight; the water translucent cyan; the clouds white. RO1 is the only dark picture.
- **No people, no text, no logos:** no workers, no radiation symbols, no reactor dome, no plant or company names anywhere.
