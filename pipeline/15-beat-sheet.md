## Set
- **Place:** a big NFL stadium in the desert on a bright, hot, clear day, shown as a cutaway model. An oval bowl of grey seats in
  tiers, clad outside in pale silver metal panels, under a white fabric roof whose two huge middle panels slide open and shut.
  The field is natural green grass with plain white yard lines (no numbers, no logos), grown in one giant steel tray that rides on
  rows of big steel wheels along 13 steel rails set in a grey concrete floor. In the right end wall of the bowl, a huge opening as
  wide as the field; the rails run straight out through it across a flat, sunny lot of pale tan desert ground, with a few saguaro
  cacti and palo verde trees, and hazy mountains far off.
- **The cut:** the near long side of the stadium is sliced away like a cake (the near stands and the ground beneath them), so the
  camera sees into the bowl from the side, and the ground under the field is cut straight down along the near sideline, toward
  the camera. The cut face, from the top: the grass and its roots; a thick layer of tan sand with fine fibres in it; a thin black
  plastic drainage mat; a white drain pipe running along the length; the steel bottom and side of the tray; a long row of big
  round steel wheels (seen side-on) on the near rail; the grey concrete floor slab; layers of pale desert soil to the bottom edge.
- **The tortoise:** one desert tortoise, outside in the lot beside a rail (TO0 only; never shown before the joke).
- **Stage ladder:**
  - S0: the field inside, the roof open; below the near edge of the grass a plain, solid face of grey concrete and pale soil: no
    tray, no wheels, no layers showing. **First frame (SIDE0) and last frame.**
  - S1 (~0.5 s on): that face has dropped away: the tray's layers and the row of wheels show. Roof open (SIDE1, WH0, RF0).
  - S1 shade: the roof closed, the field in shade, the grass pale and dull (RF1).
  - S2: the field outside on its rails in the sunny lot beside the stadium, the roof closed (HI0, RA0, TO0, LA0); inside, the bare
    concrete floor with the 13 rails (FL0), then a basketball court on it (FL1). HI1 is HI0's framing with the field still inside.
  - Passing states in the clips only: the wheels turning and the field starting to glide right (C2); the roof closing (C3); the
    field sliding out (C4); the sprinklers on and the water draining (C8, C9); the court assembling (C10).
  - Back to S1 and then S0 for the last frame: the solid face rises back into place on frame 0's framing (C14).
- **Grade:** bright, hot, clear desert daylight; inside under the closed roof, cooler and dimmer.
- **No people, no text, no logos:** no players, no crowd, no workers, no team colours, no names, no numbers anywhere.
