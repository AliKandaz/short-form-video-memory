Pinned comment: Real grass or turf? Pick one.

Replies ready:
- "Which stadium is this?" → State Farm Stadium in Glendale, Arizona, the Arizona Cardinals' home. The grass lives outside on
  most days and rolls in for games.
- "How long does it take?" → About 70 to 75 minutes to roll the roughly 740 feet in or out, at about an eighth of a mile an hour.
- "Is a tortoise really faster?" → Yes: a desert tortoise walks about 0.2 mph; the field moves at about 1/8 mph (0.125).
- "Only 76 horsepower?" → 76 of the 546 wheels have a 1-horsepower electric motor each, by the engineers who built it.
  Rolling on steel wheels along steel rails takes very little force once it's moving.
- "Why not just use turf?" → Most players and teams prefer natural grass. The tray lets the stadium keep its roof and air
  conditioning and still grow real grass in the sun.
- "Doesn't the Raiders' stadium do this?" → Yes. Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas has a field tray too, under a roof that
  never opens.
- "What about winter?" → On cold nights the field can come in too; it lives outside most days.
