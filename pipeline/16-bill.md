## Power a night ("less than a dollar a night in power")
- 51 LED strings at 4 to 7 W each: 204 to 357 W (100-count LED strings are quoted at 4–7 W, some guides say up to 8–10 W).
- The pixels: the controller box carries one 350 W supply and the arch kit has its own; at the supplies' limits about 700 W,
  far less in a normal sequence (shows usually run pixels well under full brightness).
- Five hours a night: 204 to 1,057 W × 5 h = 1.0 to 5.3 kWh. At 18.16 ¢ a kWh that is $0.19 to $0.96.
- The price of power: 18.16 ¢ a kWh, the US residential average for Aug 2026 (EIA Electric Power Monthly table 5.6.A, read
  through priceofelectricity.com and energyfactbook.com; 18.19 ¢ for Jan–Jul 2026).
- For scale, not said in the video: the same 51 strings as incandescent would draw about 2,040 W, 10.2 kWh, about $1.85 a night.
