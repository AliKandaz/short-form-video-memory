# 18 The ship launched from the back yard: the bill (Gate A draft, 25 Nov 2026)

The price counter of CLAUDE.md 4.4 for video 18 ("How to Launch a Ship From Your Back Yard"). US dollars. Every price was
read on 25 Nov 2026 **from web-search summaries of sellers' listings, cost guides and price reports**: the pages themselves
can't be opened from a session (CLAUDE.md 7.7), so nothing here has been seen on the page. The two biggest lines are worth
a look in a browser before the numbers go on screen: the boat hoist (88% of the total) and the piles. Where sources
disagree the middle is taken and the range kept. Nothing is invented: what has no price read is left out and listed at
the bottom, so the total is a floor.

**What the bill prices** (the sizes are mine, chosen to fit the picture; none comes from a source): a steel workboat about
45 ft long and about 20 tons, as long as the house. The slipway is 100 ft long, from the middle of the lawn to under the
water: two rows of six piles and two lines of rail. The drag chains are 2 tons of chain in four heaps (no source gave a
ratio of chain to hull: OSHA only says "drag chains of predetermined weights"). The ballast is 5 metric tons. The hoist is
the smallest class that lifts a boat of that weight.

**Total: $397,150.** Spoken: "about four hundred thousand dollars." On screen the final number is $397,150.

**Left out (no price read, or not ours to price):** the boat itself (the description says so); the timber cradle (kits
exist only for small boats: a 4,200 lb aluminum marine railway is $10,035 without its winch, Docks Plus); the hook and the
lever of the trigger; the winch that hauls the hull back; the hatch cover; the two concrete piers of the hoist (ordinary
piers are $100–$300 a linear foot in HomeAdvisor's and HomeGuide's guides, and a hoist pier is far heavier, so that figure
is not used); the neighbor's dock; engineering and permits.

**Arithmetic:** 50 + 36,000 + 1,900 = 37,950; + 3,000 = 40,950; + 2,700 (4,000 × 0.68 = 2,720, rounded) = 43,650;
+ 2,500 (5 × 508 = 2,540, rounded) = 46,150; + 351,000 (the middle of 335,000 and 367,000) = 397,150.
