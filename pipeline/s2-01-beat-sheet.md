## The story
- **The humble thing:** a sagging old wooden chicken coop at the bottom of a suburban back yard.
- **The impressive thing:** a cozy little guest cabin for the holidays, built on the old coop's frame; the hens get a new house of
  their own, painted to match (the joke).
- **The opening as three lines:**
  1. **Frame 0:** the sagging coop big in the frame, five hens pecking in front of it on frosty grass, the builder beside it with
     a crowbar; on the back steps of the house behind, the witness with a mug, smirking.
  2. **By 1.0 s:** the coop's sagging roof gives way and drops into it with a burst of straw and dust; the hens flap away across
     the lawn, unhurt (no hen is inside or under it).
  3. **At 1–2 s:** a cut to the witness laughing behind her mug, steam rising; then the dust settling, the builder's hand on his
     head.
- **The witness (cut B):** the builder's wife on the back steps: laughs at frame 0; her grin fades mid-video; at the end she
  stands on the steps staring at the glowing cabin. Mockery, never violence.
- **The joke:** the hens' new house is a tiny copy of the cabin (same green and cream, same little porch), and at the end a hen
  sits on the cabin's porch rail peering in through the window.
- **The last touch:** at real speed, the builder switches on the bedside lamp, the room goes warm, he plumps the pillow.
- **Caption idea for later** (our words, 8): "Her mom's coming for Christmas. The guest room used to be a chicken coop 🐔".
