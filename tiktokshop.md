## 0. The short version

1. **You do not need to "get monetized" first.** "Monetized" usually means the Creator Rewards Program, which pays per
   view and needs 10,000 followers. TikTok Shop affiliate is a separate program with a lower bar (section 1).
2. **The bar in the US is 1,000 followers, not 5,000.** You also need to be 18 or older, based in the US, and have a
   clean account. You apply yourself, inside the app (section 2).
3. **Under 5,000 followers you start in a Pilot Program** for at least 30 days: 3 product videos a day, a smaller choice
   of products, no campaigns. Full access comes at 5,000 followers (section 2.3).
4. **You are 956 followers away.** The fastest route is to get one account to 1,000 real followers and apply that day.
   At today's pace that is about 3 months; one breakout video or a small paid boost can make it days or weeks (section 4).
5. **There is no legitimate shortcut under 1,000** for selling other brands' products. Buying followers or an account
   risks the whole account (section 3).
6. **$5,000 a month is possible but rare:** roughly a top-1% result. It needs about $38,500 of sales a month, which is
   roughly 1.6 to 1.8 million views a month on product videos. Not realistic in the first months; a fair stretch goal
   for months 6 to 12 (section 5).
7. **The catch for us:** TikTok Shop wants product videos that show the real product and your face. Animated or
   AI-only videos count as low quality. Our 3D explainers can build the followers, but they cannot be the product
   videos (section 2.5).

## 1. What "monetized" means on TikTok

TikTok has several ways to earn. They are separate programs with separate rules. Joining one does not unlock another.

| Program | What it pays for | US requirements | Needed for TikTok Shop? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TikTok Shop affiliate** | A commission each time someone buys through the product link on your video | 1,000 followers, 18+, based in the US, clean account (section 2) | **This is the one** |
| **Creator Rewards Program** | Views on videos of one minute or longer | 10,000 followers, 100,000 views in the last 30 days, 18+, a personal account (official) | **No** |

- So the goal is not "get monetized, then join TikTok Shop". The goal is **1,000 followers, then apply to TikTok Shop**.
- Both programs can run on the same account. Our ~75-second explainers already fit Creator Rewards on length; that
  program is covered in `CLAUDE.md` section 6.9.
- Other ways to earn (LIVE gifts, subscriptions, brand deals) were not researched here.

## 2. The rules for TikTok Shop affiliate in the US

Checked on 13 Nov 2026. The newest version of TikTok's Creator Eligibility Policy that the search found is dated
25 Sep 2026. **The eligibility screen inside your own TikTok app is the final word**: TikTok has changed these rules
several times.

### 2.1 Who can join (official)

- **At least 1,000 followers** to apply yourself as an "Affiliate Creator".
- **18 or older.** You confirm your age within two months of signing up.
- **Based in the United States.**
- **A clean account:** it follows the Community Guidelines and TikTok Shop's content rules, and it has never had its
  Shop permissions taken away.
- **Not** a government, politician or political party account.
- **Identity check:** full name, date of birth, address and a government ID. It is usually asked for after your first
  commission and usually takes minutes (up to 24 hours). One ID can verify up to five creator accounts.

*Why you may have heard 5,000:* that was the old entry bar, and it is still the line for full access. Many 2025 guides
still repeat it. One blog claims the 1,000 entry is being pulled back to 5,000 "for most users"; TikTok's own policy
text does not say that, but check your app.

### 2.2 The three kinds of creator account (official)

| Type | How you get it | Follower minimum | What you can sell |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Affiliate Creator** (what we want) | You apply yourself in the app | **1,000** | Any product in the Product Marketplace (in the Pilot: only from shops scoring 95% or higher) |
| **Marketing Creator** | A seller invites your account to its shop (a "marketing bind") | None to be attached; 5,000 before you can also use the marketplace | That one seller's products |
| **Official Shop Creator** | A seller attaches its own brand account, with the same name as the shop | None | Only that shop's products; no marketplace |

A seller gets very few of these attachments: one official account, plus up to four marketing accounts once the shop has
passed its own probation.

### 2.3 The Pilot Program: what happens between 1,000 and 5,000 followers (official)

Everyone approved with fewer than 5,000 followers is put in the "Affiliate Creator Pilot Program" for at least 30 days.

```
Today: 44 followers
   |   follower sprint (section 4)
   v
1,000 followers  -->  apply in the app  -->  approved (about 1-3 business days, third-party)
   |
   v
PILOT, at least 30 days
   - up to 3 shoppable videos a day and 3 shoppable LIVEs a week
   - only products from shops with a Shop Performance Score of 95% or higher
   - no campaigns
   |   early unlock: pass the quiz + Creator Health Rating 176 or more + one task
   |   (lifts most limits; the 3-videos-a-day cap appears to stay)
   v
5,000 followers AND 30 days in the program  -->  FULL ACCESS
   - up to 30 shoppable videos and 60 shoppable photo posts a day
   - campaigns, every shop
```

- **A "shoppable" video** is a video with a product link on it. Videos without a link are not limited.
- **The task for the early unlock** is one of: 6 or more shoppable videos of at least 8 seconds each, one LIVE of at
  least 5 minutes, or 10 orders. The unlock is at TikTok's discretion.
- **Avoid the "Extended Pilot" tag.** TikTok can put an account on a stricter tier (3 shoppable videos a *week*, 1 LIVE a
  week) and it cannot graduate while tagged. Follow the content rules from the first video.

### 2.4 Money and account health (official unless marked)

- **Commission:** each seller sets its own rate. Open offers usually pay about 10–15%; a widely repeated US average is
  13% (third-party, method not shown). Two trade sites report cuts in mid-2026, one saying the top rate for creators
  under 500,000 followers fell from 20% to 15% in eight categories. TikTok's own pages did not confirm it in my search.
- **When you get paid:** usually on the 15th day after the order is delivered, and up to 31 days. A refunded order pays
  nothing. So the first money arrives about a month after the first sale.
- **Tax:** you file a Form W-9 in the app. Without it TikTok withholds 24% of payments. Commission is taxable income, so
  keep part of it aside (this is not tax advice).
- **Creator Health Rating:** a score from 0 to 1,000. You start at 200. Breaking a rule removes points. 200 or more is
  healthy, 151–199 needs improvement, 150 or less is at risk, and 0 removes Shop access for good.
- **Free samples (third-party, sources disagree):** usually tied to 5,000 followers or to recent sales. In the Pilot,
  expect to buy the products yourself or use refundable samples.

### 2.5 Content rules that matter most for us

From TikTok Shop's "Requirements for High-Quality Videos and LIVEs" and its AI-generated content policy (official).

| Rule | What it means for us |
|---|---|
| Product videos should be dynamic and "feature your face and the physical product", with at least 3 seconds of moving footage. | You film the real product. A voice over pictures is not enough. |
| No still, looping or scrolling images through the whole video without your face and the product. "Still-frame content" includes screenshots, slideshows, screen recordings and **animated content**. | **Our 3D AI explainers do not qualify as product videos.** |
| AI is allowed for editing, styling, translation and scripting. It must not mislead, impersonate anyone, or change or exaggerate the product (size, colour, material, quantity, features, instant results). | AI can help write and edit. It cannot stand in for the product. |
| Disclose AI when a video is fully generated or heavily edited with it (the "AI-generated content" switch when posting, or a label on screen). | Switch it on whenever AI made a real part of the video. |
| LIVEs: no AI or pre-recorded voices; stills or animated figures may not cover more than half the screen. | LIVE selling means you, live. |

Not settled:
- **AI voice-over on a recorded product video.** The official text ties the voice ban to LIVEs; blogs disagree about
  recorded videos. Treat it as a risk until checked in the app's policy pages.
- **Hands-only videos (no face).** TikTok's page asks for your face. Third-party guides say hands-only demos of the
  real product are common and pass. On-camera is the safe choice.

5. **Free extras:** let TikTok suggest the account to your phone contacts, and ask people you know to follow.

## 5. How realistic is $5,000 a month?

### 5.1 The math

| Step | Number | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Commission wanted | $5,000 a month | The goal |
| Average commission rate | about 13% | Third-party; open offers usually 10–15% |
| Sales you must cause | **about $38,500 a month** ($33,300 at 15%, $50,000 at 10%) | $5,000 ÷ rate |
| Orders, at $35 an order | about 1,100 a month, about 37 a day | $38,500 ÷ $35 |
| Orders per 1,000 views | about 0.6 (2% tap the product, 3% of those buy) | A calculator model, third-party |
| Commission per 1,000 views | about $3 (reported range $1 to $15+) | 0.6 × $35 × 13–15% |
| **Views needed on product videos** | **about 1.6–1.8 million a month** (5 million at $1; 500,000 at $10) | $5,000 ÷ $3 per 1,000 |
| In the Pilot (90 product videos a month) | each video must average about 18,000–20,000 views | 1.6–1.8M ÷ 90 |

For scale: our TikTok videos average about 5,800 views today, and half of them are under about 1,200. A view on a
product video is worth more than a Creator Rewards view: about $3 per 1,000 views against $0.40–1.00 (third-party
figures).

### 5.2 Who actually earns what

There is no verified earnings table for affiliates. One analysis found that most figures online trace back to
unsourced blog posts. Read these as direction only:

- **A few accounts take most of the money.** The top 200 creators are said to drive 31% of affiliate sales, and the top
  0.5% (about 4,000 accounts) 38%.
- **Typical earnings are small.** Blogs put most active affiliates at $50–500 a month, and say more than half quit
  within a year.
- **The market is growing.** US TikTok Shop sales are estimated at about $15.8 billion in 2025 and $11.8 billion in the
  first half of 2026, with affiliates driving somewhere between 40% and 75% of it.
- **My estimate from those numbers:** roughly 1 affiliate in 100, or fewer, earns $5,000 a month. Many of the rest
  post rarely or have quit, so someone who posts every day for a year is not competing with the whole crowd.

### 5.3 What works for us and against us

| For us | Against us |
|---|---|
| The entry bar is low (1,000) and the market is growing. | Product videos must show the real product and you. Our AI pipeline does not carry over, so filming is a new skill. |
| We already know hooks, scripts, captions and covers. | The Pilot caps us at 3 product videos a day, on a smaller choice of shops, until 5,000 followers. |
| A product video pays roughly 3 to 7 times more per view than Creator Rewards (third-party figures). | Products cost money at first: free samples usually need sales history or 5,000 followers. |
| Blogs say more than half of affiliates quit within a year. | Refunds cancel commission, pay arrives 15–31 days after delivery, and commission rates were reportedly cut in 2026. |

### 5.4 Verdict

**Possible, but not a fair expectation for the first six months.** It is roughly a top-1% result.

A realistic ladder (my estimates, counted from the day the account is approved):

| When | Realistic monthly commission | What it takes |
|---|---|---|
| Months 1–2 | $0–300 | Learning to film products. The first payout usually lands in month two (third-party). |
| Months 3–6 | $300–1,500 | A product video every day and one product that sells. |
| Months 6–12 | $1,500–5,000 | Out of the Pilot (5,000 followers), two or three winning products, several videos a month over 100,000 views. |
| $5,000 and up, every month | — | About 1.6 million or more product-video views every month. Most affiliates never reach it. |

A sensible first target is $500–1,500 a month within six months of approval, with $5,000 as the 12-month stretch goal.
